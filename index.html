Why it matters here

The Philippines sits on the Pacific Ring of Fire and along the typhoon belt. Zamboanga City faces the Sulu Sea and the Moro Gulf, so earthquakes, tsunamis, floods and fires all deserve a plan.

Ring of Fire

Frequent earthquakes and volcanoes shape the whole country, including Mindanao.

Typhoon belt

Around 20 tropical cyclones enter the Philippine area each year. Mindanao is hit less often, but heavy rain and flooding still happen.

Coastal city

Low-lying barangays and shoreline communities need to know their evacuation routes to high ground.

Civil emergencies

The 2013 Zamboanga crisis showed that families may need to evacuate fast for reasons other than weather.

Typhoon wind signals

PAGASA raises Tropical Cyclone Wind Signals (TCWS) based on expected wind speed. A higher number means stronger winds and less time to prepare.

Signal 1Strong winds, 39–61 km/h. Secure loose items, stay updated.~36 hrs ahead
Signal 2Gale-force, 62–88 km/h. Light damage to weak houses likely.~24 hrs ahead
Signal 3Storm-force, 89–117 km/h. Move to a sturdy shelter.~18 hrs ahead
Signal 4Typhoon-force, 118–184 km/h. Severe, widespread damage.~12 hrs ahead
Signal 5185 km/h or more. Catastrophic. Stay inside a safe shelter.~12 hrs ahead

Make a family plan

Do this together tonight. It takes about thirty minutes.

Five steps

  1. Find your barangay’s evacuation center and the safest route to it.
  2. Choose a meeting place near home and another outside your neighborhood.
  3. Pick one relative in another city as the family contact.
  4. Keep copies of IDs, land papers and medical records in a waterproof bag.
  5. Practice a drill twice a year, including with children and elders.

Look after everyone

  1. Write down medicines and doses for family members who need them.
  2. Plan how to move elders, people with disabilities and young children.
  3. Include pets: food, water, a leash or carrier.
  4. Agree to text instead of calling when networks are crowded.
  5. Charge power banks when a storm or heavy rain is forecast.
